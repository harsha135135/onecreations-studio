## PCIe link

- **Topology** (from Windows PnP location paths):
  - The RTX 4070 is on CPU root port 00:01.0, "PCIe RC 010 G5" (ACPI PEG1). That is the CPU PCIe 5.0 x16 slot, PCIEX16(G5).
  - The NVMe is on the separate CPU root port 00:06.0 (ACPI PEG0, VMD-managed). That is M.2_1, which has its own Gen4 x4 CPU lanes.
  - No second CPU PEG root port (00:01.1) is present. That suggests the x16 slot is not configured to bifurcate x8/x8. This is an inference.
- ASUS's published spec (PCIe 5.0 x16 slot from the CPU; M.2_1 from the CPU with Gen4 x4; M.2_2 and M.2_3 plus three x16-size slots in x4 mode from the
  chipset) lists **no lane sharing** between the x16 slot and any M.2 slot.
  Source: <https://www.asus.com/us/motherboards-components/motherboards/prime/prime-z790-p-wifi/techspec/>.
- **Under load**, Cycles OptiX rendered the diagnostic scene at 1920×1080 with 4096 samples while `nvidia-smi` sampled every 250 ms. Raw data is in
  `2026-10-02-desktop-m1-pcie-load.csv`. There were 285 busy samples over about 71 s (GPU utilisation median 93 %, 131 W, 2880 MHz, P2), and
  **every one was Gen4 x4**. At idle the link was gen1 x4. The card and slot both support x16 (max width 16).
- **Interpretation (proposed, unverified):** a Gen4 card in the CPU x16 slot that trains at x4 under load is not explained by M.2 sharing on this
  board. The candidates to check without changing settings are:
  - card seating, or debris or damage in the slot
  - a riser cable, if the card is vertically mounted
  - the BIOS PCIe slot configuration (look at it only; do not change it)
  - the card itself

  Gen4 x4 gives about 7.9 GB/s against about 31.5 GB/s at x16. These are arithmetic figures from the spec rates, not measured bandwidth. The gap matters for M3 CPU-offload rungs.
  **No BIOS PCIe settings were changed** (owner decision).
- **After the iGPU reboot, the link is x16.** The same render and sampling were repeated at 10:30, with raw data in `2026-10-02-desktop-m1-pcie-load-after.csv`.
  - All 273 busy samples over about 68 s were **Gen4 x16** (utilisation median 93 %, 134 W, 2880 MHz; render time 71.6 s). At idle the link was gen1 x16.
  - Windows PnP reports CurrentLinkWidth = 16 as well.
  - No PCIe setting was changed, so **the cause of the earlier x4 is not identified**. Plausible explanations, none verified:
    - The link retrained on a full power cycle.
    - The card was nudged when the DisplayPort cable was unplugged from it.
    - The link trained marginally on the earlier boot.
  - Treat the x4 state as an **intermittent risk**. Record the link gen and width with every admission run (`tools/admission/monitor.py` does not capture them yet).
