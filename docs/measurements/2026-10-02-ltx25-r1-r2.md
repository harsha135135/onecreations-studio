### Findings

1. **LTX-2.5 22B runs on this 4070 + 32 GB machine at the R1/R2 envelope.** It is fast (about 31 s cold, about 8–9 s warm, end to end), deterministic per seed, and reliable for every run so far (6 of 6 jobs). This is the "do not assume it fails on 32 GB" check from ADR-0001, now measured.
2. **The binding resource is system RAM, not VRAM or the pagefile.**
   - Free RAM fell to about **2.0 GB** of 31.75 GB while the transformer staged.
   - The process tree reached about 22 GB working set and about 35 GB private bytes; pinned memory reserves 13 GB.
   - Commit peaked at **74–75 %** of the 60 GB limit.
   - Pagefile use stayed at **69–156 MB**, so there was **no sustained paging**.
   - *Proposed:* larger envelopes (more frames or pixels) will press on RAM first. The pagefile stays system-managed per the owner's decision, and the monitor's `commit_near_limit` and `paging_suspected` flags will show when that changes.
3. **The first denoise step costs about 12 s even with a warm file cache.** R1b (warm cache) had a first step of 11.5 s against 11.9 s cold, so this is DynamicVRAM staging the 20.5 GB INT8 transformer into pinned memory and the GPU, not disk reads. Warm jobs in the same process avoid most of it (first step 1.8–2.4 s).
4. **Sysmem fallback is not used.** The R1b outputs are **byte-identical** to R1a's (same SHA-256; PSNR ∞) with no OOM and similar memory and timing. ComfyUI's DynamicVRAM keeps residency within the 12 GB itself. The device-wide VRAM peak of about 11.9 GB is above the v1 proposed 11,000 MB target **by design** of DynamicVRAM, not as an overflow.
5. **PCIe stays at Gen4 x16.** It dropped to **gen2 x16** for about 1–5 s in every run, at 20–28 W board power and 50–100 % "utilisation". That is the transfer-bound weight-streaming phase at the start of denoising, where the GPU stays in a lower power state; the link returns to Gen4 as soon as compute ramps. The width never dropped: the earlier x4 issue did not recur. *Proposed:* the effect on first-step time is unmeasured; it would need a run with the link held at Gen4.
6. **R2 control: partial.** The owner's playback review found motion in both clips, but it reads as vague and its direction is hard to tell. That review is the outcome.
   A frame-sheet check (frames 0/12/24/36/48 against the depth control) shows the keyframe positions tracking the control.
   - The cube's translation, the cone's turn, the occluder pillar crossing at frame 24 and the camera dolly/rise all follow the Blender depth pass frame by frame, for both seeds.
   - The first-frame look (gray studio) is preserved.
   - The depth convention (inverse planar depth, p1–p99 per clip, near = white) is still *proposed*.
   - **Candidate reasons for the vague read (proposed, none tested):**
     - the diagnostic scene is small, low-contrast and untextured gray
     - the clip is short: 2 s / 49 frames
     - 512×320 is the admission envelope
     - the depth convention is unverified
     - stage 1 only
   - These need a measured comparison before any change.
7. **Quality** (owner, R1): coherent and on-prompt, with good backgrounds. Faces are unclear and overall clarity is limited at 512×320, which is the admission envelope, not the model's ceiling. Resolution, length and the official two-stage upscaler are later rungs and need owner approval plus one more download (the spatial upscaler, 1 GB).
