# M2-B same-shot resolution comparison: 512×320 → 768×448 — 2026-10-03

**Question:** does increasing **only** the spatial output resolution (512×320 → 768×448) materially improve preservation of the fast-moving mace, while keeping M2-B's proven articulation and timing?

**Answer: NO MATERIAL IMPROVEMENT** (pre-registered classification, one generation, `ltx25-m2b-strike-768`).
- 768×448 ran cleanly on the 4070 / 32 GB.
- It kept the timing (apex and contact on the authored frames), the articulation and the planted root, and it sharpened static and slow detail.
- But the fast-strike weapon failure is essentially unchanged:
  - the same frame (f25) is still a semi-transparent ghost
  - fast-region retention has the same median (0.568 vs 0.570)
  - the shaft is still missing through the worst frames
- Under the M2-B shot rule, the 768 clip would still be PARTIAL.

All figures are **measured**, from one generation per resolution, unless labelled otherwise. The 512 baseline is the original-speed run `ltx25-m2b-strike` (`docs/measurements/2026-10-03-m2b-temple-strike.md`). The slowed retry appears only as secondary context.

## 1. Why 768×448

- It is the next quality-ladder step that both M2-A and M2-B docs proposed: no upscaler, and 960×544 is deferred.
- It is the smallest increase that tests whether more spatial representation preserves a fast thin weapon.
- RAM was the known risk at 512×320 (about 2.05 GB minimum free), so this run is also a compute-admission test.

**Resolution-derived consequences** (normal pipeline behaviour, documented rather than changed):
- **Aspect 1.600 → 1.714, horizontal FOV kept:** the AUTO sensor fit is horizontal for landscape, so the vertical FOV goes from 35.6° to 33.4°. The 768 frame equals the 512 frame scaled ×1.5 with 16 px trimmed top and bottom (verified on the first frames). Nothing in the subject is cropped: the gate's in-frame check holds on all 49 frames.
- **Depth normalisation is unchanged:** near 4.2024 m, far ≈ 23.19 m, identical inverse-depth range. The background fraction drops from 5.4 % to 4.3 % because less sky is in view.
- **Decode tiling:** the official `VAEDecodeTiled` (tile 512, overlap 64) is a single tile at 512×320 but genuinely tiles at 768×448. No tile seams were seen.

## 3. Union control grid at each resolution (derived from the pinned code)

Sources: ComfyUI `6b747c0` and ComfyUI-LTXVideo `3bf3ca62`.
- `EmptyLTXVLatentVideo` makes a latent of (W/32) × (H/32), with (49 − 1)/8 + 1 = 7 frames.
- The LTX video VAE's `downscale_index_formula` is (8, 32, 32).
- `LTXAddVideoICLoRAGuide.encode`:
  - resizes the control (bilinear, crop disabled) to latent × 32 / `latent_downscale_factor`, where the factor is 2 (the LoRA's `reference_downscale_factor`)
  - VAE-encodes it
  - dilates it by 2 onto the latent grid, after checking that latent W and H are divisible by 2

| | 512×320 | 768×448 |
|---|---|---|
| Latent grid | 16 × 10 | 24 × 14 |
| Guide resize (px) | 256 × 160 | 384 × 224 |
| **Guide cells** | **8 × 5** | **12 × 7** |
| Output px per guide cell | 64 | 64 (exact division at both) |

So the cell size in output pixels is the same, and the same object covers 1.5× more cells per axis at 768. The same motion also covers 1.5× more cells per frame. Tested in `tests/test_resolution_compare.py`; the helper is `tools/admission/resolution_compare.py`.

## 8. Classification (pre-registered rules): **NO MATERIAL IMPROVEMENT**

- **Not COMPUTE-NOT-ADMITTED:** a valid output, pagefile +8 MB, 37.7 s.
- **Not REGRESSION:** all guards hold (timing and position checks followed, articulation not worse, temple coherent). Fast median 0.568 ≥ 0.470, and 1 severe frame, not ≥ 2.
- **Not STRONG:** the worst frame is 0.117 (< 0.50), the median 0.568 (< 0.75), there is a severe frame, f25 is a ghost, and the shaft is not continuous in any of f25–f27.
- **Not MODEST:** none of the three conditions is met:
  - "no severe frame in the fast region": f25 is still 0.117
  - "fast median ≥ 0.670": it is 0.568
  - "shaft continuous in ≥ 1 worst-region frame": f27 has only a partial stub

The M2-B shot classification of the 768 clip would still be **PARTIAL** (`records.jsonl` `outcome`).

## 9. Conservative conclusion

**At this configuration** (LTX-2.5 22B distilled INT8, Union IC-LoRA, 49 frames at 24 fps, 8 steps, seed 1000, this scene), raising the output resolution from 512×320 to 768×448 does **not** materially improve preservation of this fast articulated weapon:
- The failure stays on the same frames, at the same median retention, with the shaft still missing.
- Static and slow detail improve.
- Timing, articulation and the planted root are fully retained.

This **strengthens, but does not prove,** the working hypothesis that the dominant limitation is temporal or generative rather than insufficient spatial representation. It is one comparison at one seed.

**Structural observation (measured fact; its causal role is a proposed hypothesis):**
- With the VAE's 8× temporal compression, output frame 0 is encoded alone and frames 8k−7 … 8k form latent frame k.
- The whole downswing f25 → contact f29 falls inside **one** temporal latent (f25–f32), and the apex f23–24 ends the previous one.
- This is the same at both resolutions; spatial resolution cannot change it.
- Whether intra-latent-frame motion of this size drives the ghosting is **untested**.

**Still a local working hypothesis, not a model limit:** "smear above roughly 0.7 cell / 45 px per frame" (from M2-B) is **not** a general LTX-2.5 threshold. The 768 run shows that raw px/frame and cells/frame are not the right normalisation on their own: the speed in head widths per frame (1.25) was unchanged, and so was the failure.

## 10. Implication for weapon-critical frames

- Weapon-critical fast frames should keep **deterministic Blender geometry/compositing as the candidate route** (hard rule 8). The joints and timing are already exact (0.57–0.79 px, 0/0 frames).
- That is a valid part of the intended hybrid architecture, not an experiment failure.
- 768×448 stays a **quality** candidate for the representative shot: sharper environment and slow subject detail, admitted at about +16 % time with no extra RAM. It is not a fix for fast weapons.
